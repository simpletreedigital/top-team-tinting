Common Questions

Residential Tinting, Answered

Will residential tint block my ocean view?

No. Top Team offers a range of film clarity levels, from nearly invisible heat-rejecting film to decorative and privacy options, so you keep the view while cutting heat and glare.

Can you tint sliding lanai doors?

Yes, lanai sliders and large glass doors are some of the most common residential jobs Top Team handles on Kauai.

Does tint really protect furniture from fading?

Yes. Residential film blocks the majority of UV rays that cause flooring, furniture, and artwork to fade over time.
